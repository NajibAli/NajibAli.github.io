How to play

Two players share this device. Take turns dropping a disc into one of the seven columns. It falls to the lowest free space. The first to line up four discs in a row, across, down or diagonally, wins.

Keyboard: use the arrow keys to choose a column and Enter to drop, or press 1 to 7.
