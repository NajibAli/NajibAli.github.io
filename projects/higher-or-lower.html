    How to play
    • You start with 10 credits.
    • Roll to get a starting number, then guess if the next roll will be higher or lower.
    • A correct guess earns 5 points. A tie counts as correct.
    • A wrong guess costs 1 credit and resets your streak.
    • Guess right 5 times in a row to unlock a bonus spin. It adds a random number from 5 to 25, multiplied by 10, to your score.
    • The game ends when you run out of credits. Then you can add your name to the leaderboard.
