How to play
  • Hit a mole to score 10 points.
  • Don't hit a bomb. That ends the game.
  • You have 60 seconds.
  • In the last 20 seconds, everything moves twice as fast.
  • Your best score is saved on this device.
  • Keyboard: S to start, Space to pause, M to mute, 1 to 9 to hit a hole.
